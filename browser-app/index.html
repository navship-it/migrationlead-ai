A consultant’s daily routine

01 · Bring in your leads

Export an anonymised CSV from Excel using the template below. Import replaces the browser’s current list after confirmation.

02 · Review the queue

Filter for priority and check each reason. Scores use an enquiry’s first 48-hour snapshot. First contact should happen promptly, before this scoring point.

03 · Contact the customer

Use your normal call or messaging tools. Open a lead to record its current status, consultant and next follow-up date.

04 · Save the updated worklist

Export all statuses to carry updates back to Excel. There is no automatic workbook sync or messaging. Download a backup before clearing browser data.

Explainable scoring

Consultation booked: 40 points
At least 3 interactions: 30 points
Engaged in last 12 hours: 20 points
Initial response over 12 hours: 10 points for recovery attention.

High: 60–100 · Medium: 30–59
Standard: below 30

Points are provisional business rules, not percentages. Snapshot fields remain historical; current status and follow-up dates are maintained separately. Closed leads leave the active queue. Keep a minimum service standard for every lead.
